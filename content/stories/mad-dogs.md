Mad Dogs is one of the paintings that made Jack Vettriano's name.

He painted it in 1991, early in his professional career. Fashionably dressed figures with umbrellas stand on a beach, in strong, dramatic light. The title comes from Noël Coward's famous quip, that only mad dogs and Englishmen go out in the midday sun, and the painting shares Coward's wit: people determined to keep up appearances, whatever the weather.

It is witty, stylish and beautifully composed, everything that made his early beach paintings so loved. Years later, it was chosen for the major retrospective of his work at Kelvingrove Art Gallery and Museum in Glasgow, and it remains one of the images collectors ask us for most often.

This signed edition is limited to three hundred and fifty prints, with forty Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, on Hahnemühle mould-made paper. It is a wonderful way to own a signed version of one of his most celebrated paintings. And it belongs on a bright wall.
