Narcissistic Bathers is one of the most significant Vettriano releases of recent years, and not only because of the image.

It belongs to a small group of previously unreleased editions that Jack Vettriano signed by hand before his death in March 2025. That makes it one of the very last editions anywhere to carry his original pencil signature. When it was released in September 2026, there was never any doubt that collectors would take notice.

The title is pure Vettriano: knowing, a little provocative, suggesting figures more absorbed in themselves, and in how they look, than in anything around them. Bathers by the sea are one of the oldest subjects in painting. Vettriano brings his own theatrical, cinematic sense of style to it, and turns a simple seaside scene into something charged with self-regard and quiet drama.

The edition is limited to one hundred and twenty-five prints and fifteen Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered. Signed editions released after an artist's death are rare by definition. There will be no more of them.
