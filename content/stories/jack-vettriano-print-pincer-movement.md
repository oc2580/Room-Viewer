Pincer Movement borrows its title from military strategy: the manoeuvre in which an opponent is surrounded from two sides at once.

Vettriano applies the idea, with typical wit, to the battlefield of romance and seduction. His titles often cast relationships as games, or campaigns, and this one suggests a carefully planned approach. But as in any campaign, the outcome is far from certain. Who is surrounding whom? And who will surrender first?

It is a later image, published in 2012, and its clever title and strong composition make it a real talking point. Hang it in a living room or a study, and wait for your guests to ask about the name.

The edition is one of the smaller runs among his signed prints: just one hundred, plus ten Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, on heavyweight museum etching paper. This example is framed and ready to hang.
