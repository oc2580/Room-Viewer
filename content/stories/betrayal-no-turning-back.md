Betrayal: No Turning Back. A man in a dark suit holds a woman close in a dim room, her dress slipping from her shoulder.

If First Kiss is where the story begins, this is the moment it becomes irreversible. The title leaves no room for doubt. A line has been crossed, and both of them know it.

Vettriano was a master of this kind of moment: not the event itself, but the instant just before or just after, when everything is still charged. The dim light, the dark suit, the closeness of the figures: every element is staged with the precision of a film still.

The original oil on canvas was painted in 2001 and shown that year at the International Art and Design Fair in New York. It has since sold. This print, from an edition of only one hundred, plus ten Artist's Proofs, is signed and numbered by the artist, and it is the natural companion to Betrayal: First Kiss.
