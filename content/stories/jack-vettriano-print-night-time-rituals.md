Night-Time Rituals explores a theme Vettriano returned to all through his career: the private habits and ceremonies that come with the evening.

Dressing. Undressing. Pouring a drink. Waiting for a knock at the door. In his paintings these ordinary acts become charged and theatrical, lit as though for a film. The word ritual suggests something familiar, repeated many times. But it also suggests something with its own rules, and that tension lies at the heart of his intimate interiors.

This painting belongs to the more sensual strand of his work, and it is best enjoyed close up, in a private room: a bedroom, or a dressing room.

It is also a Premium Limited Edition, which means it is presented very differently from a paper print. It is reproduced at the exact size of the original painting, board mounted and finished by hand with three coats of lacquer, for a satin surface closer to canvas than paper. It arrives in the same style of frame Vettriano used for his originals, with no border and no glass, and each one is signed and numbered by him in silver pen. A premium edition is the closest a print can come to the presence of the original canvas.
