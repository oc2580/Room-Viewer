Cocktails and Broken Hearts. A late-night bar, its figures gathered at the counter in low, smoky light.

The title does the rest. It hints at the stories behind the drinks: who is waiting for whom, who has been let down, and who is about to be. Vettriano never spells it out. He sets the scene, dims the lights, and lets us eavesdrop.

The original oil on canvas was painted in 1998 and shown that year in Between Darkness and Dawn, one of his exhibitions. It was chosen again, years later, for Jack Vettriano: A Retrospective, at Kelvingrove Art Gallery and Museum in Glasgow, in 2013 and 2014. That tells you how highly it was regarded.

This edition is a silkscreen, published in 2003, limited to two hundred and seventy-five, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano. Silkscreens are built up colour by colour, by hand, and they have a depth and intensity all of their own. At seventy by fifty-six centimetres, it is a substantial image, made for an evening room.
