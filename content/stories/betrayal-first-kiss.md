Betrayal: First Kiss. A man and a woman share a first kiss in a softly lit room, wall lights glowing on either side of them.

This painting belongs to a small group Vettriano made on the theme of betrayal, alongside Betrayal: No Turning Back, and Contemplation of Betrayal. Taken together, they read almost like the frames of a film. And this is where the story begins.

Notice how much the title changes what you see. Without it, this is a tender, romantic moment. With it, the same kiss carries a shadow: somewhere outside this room, someone is about to be hurt. That tension between beauty and consequence is the very heart of Vettriano's work.

The edition is small, just one hundred prints and ten Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano. For a collector, the real pleasure is in the series: hung together, the Betrayal paintings tell their story in full.
