Night Geometry is one of Vettriano's most cinematic compositions.

A couple embrace beside a window at night. And the title asks us to notice something we might otherwise only feel: how carefully the whole picture has been built. The lines of the window, the angles of the two bodies, the play of light and shadow, all fit together like a film set, lit and framed for a single shot.

Vettriano often spoke of the influence of film noir on his work, and here the debt is plain to see. This is a love scene composed like a frame from a black-and-white thriller, but in his own rich colour.

It captures a private moment, set against the city outside, a theme that runs through his night-time interiors: two people, and the whole world held at bay beyond the glass. The mood is sophisticated and urban, and it suits a living room or a bedroom.

Each print in the edition is signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, and like all his signed editions, the supply is now fixed. We can tell you the edition number of the example we hold.
