The Missing Man II returns to one of Vettriano's best-known titles.

The original Missing Man, published as a silkscreen in 1998, is one of his classic beach scenes. This second painting comes back to the idea at its heart: absence. Someone who should be there, and is not. And the people left behind, waiting, or wondering.

It is a wonderful device, and Vettriano used it brilliantly. With a single title, an ordinary gathering becomes a mystery. We find ourselves scanning the picture for the one person we cannot see, and asking where he has gone, and why.

The Missing Man II was released on the twenty-fifth of September 2020, as part of a celebration of Jack Vettriano's greatest works. It is reproduced at the full size of the original painting, thirty by twenty-four inches, board mounted and finished by hand with three coats of lacquer for a satin surface. It comes in the same style of frame as an original, with no border and no glass.

The edition is just seventy-five, plus five Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Vettriano in silver pen. It has the scale to anchor a room, and the story to hold your attention for years.
