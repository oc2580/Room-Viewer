The Sailor's Toy is one of the newest images in the Vettriano canon, published after the artist's death in March 2025 as an official posthumous edition.

Listen to the title for a moment. It has that wry, double-edged quality Vettriano loved: a hint of a story between two people, and no promise about who is playing with whom. That is how his best work operates. A single moment, staged as carefully as a film still, where the clothes, the setting and the body language suggest far more than they ever show.

Because the artist can no longer sign, each print in this edition of just one hundred is numbered and embossed with the official stamp of the Estate of Jack Vettriano. That stamp, with the edition number and the certificate of authenticity, records exactly where your print sits in the run.

It is a smaller, intimate format, printed on heavy museum etching paper, and it rewards being seen up close: a study, a hallway, a bedroom wall. And for a collector already building a Vettriano wall, it is a quiet way to add one of the very last images to be released.
