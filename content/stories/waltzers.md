Waltzers. Couples dance beneath the trees on a summer night, the women in pale evening gowns, the men in dark suits.

This is one of Vettriano's most romantic outdoor scenes, all movement and starlight. Dancing runs right through his work. He painted it on beaches, in ballrooms and in quiet rooms, and it stood, for him, for a kind of elegance and courtship that belongs to another era.

Here, the contrast does the work: pale gowns against dark suits, figures turning against the night. You can almost feel the warmth of the evening and hear the music drifting between the trees.

The edition was published in 2014: three hundred and fifty prints and thirty-five Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano. It is a compact, joyful image, and a lovely way to bring some of that summer-night romance into a home.
