Lucky Seven. A gambler's title, the kind Vettriano loved, with its suggestion of chance, of risk, and of an evening that might turn either way.

Games, wagers and risk recur all through his work, from card tables to dance halls. But his figures rarely seem to be playing for money. The stakes are emotional. And the number seven, traditionally the lucky one, gives the title a hopeful ring, one his paintings seldom let us take entirely at face value.

It is a smaller image, and it suits a study, a dressing room, or a group of his prints hung together. It is also one of the more accessible signed editions in our collection, a sensible place to begin, or a thoughtful gift for someone who already loves his work.

The edition is just one hundred prints and ten Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, on museum etching paper. An edition of one hundred is small by the standards of his published prints, and now that no new signed editions can ever be produced, its supply is fixed.
