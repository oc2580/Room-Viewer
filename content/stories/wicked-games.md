Wicked Games was published in November 2020, and it is one of the later signed editions released in Vettriano's lifetime.

The title, borrowed from the language of pop songs and film, sets up exactly the kind of story he loved to tell: a game of attraction and power between two people, played by rules only they understand. We are not told who is winning. That is left, as always, to us.

His later work kept the cinematic glamour of his earlier paintings, but it often became more intimate and pared back, and you can feel that here. It is a stylish, compact image, at home in a bedroom, a dressing room or a study.

The edition is small: just one hundred prints and ten Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano. That makes it relatively scarce. And for a collection weighted towards his better-known images of the 1990s, it adds real range: a glimpse of the artist in his final years.
