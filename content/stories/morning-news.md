Morning News. A woman in a blue dress reads the morning paper at an outdoor café table, a classic car parked beside her.

It is a picture of pure, unhurried pleasure. Coffee, the newspaper, the morning sun, and a beautiful car waiting at the kerb. If so many of Vettriano's paintings are about the drama of the night, this one is about the calm of the morning after, when there is nowhere to be and nothing to hurry for.

And yet, he is Vettriano. We cannot help wondering what she is reading, whose car it is, and whether the news on the page has anything to do with her.

Morning News was first shown in 2010, in Days of Wine and Roses, the exhibition at Kirkcaldy Museum and Art Gallery. That was the gallery where, as a young man, he had taught himself to paint. The original painting has since sold.

This limited edition giclée was published in 2012, each print signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano.
