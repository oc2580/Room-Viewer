Bluebird at Bonneville is one of Vettriano's most ambitious, and most distinctive, paintings.

It takes us to the golden age of the land speed record. Sir Malcolm Campbell brought his Blue Bird to the Bonneville Salt Flats in Utah, and in 1935 he became the first person to drive at more than three hundred miles an hour. Vettriano was fascinated by that era, its daring, its engineering, its glamour. And the vast white expanse of the salt flats gives this picture a scale and an openness that is rare in his work.

It is loved by motor-racing enthusiasts as much as by Vettriano collectors, and it has a companion in Pendine Beach, another of his paintings set at a famous land speed venue.

This premium edition was released as a celebration of his greatest works. It is reproduced at the full size of the original painting, twenty-four by forty inches, board mounted and finished by hand with three coats of lacquer, then framed exactly as an original would be, with no border and no glass. Just seventy-five were made, plus seven Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Vettriano in silver pen. It is one of the most significant premium editions he ever released.
