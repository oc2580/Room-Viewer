The Summer Days Triptych brings together three of Vettriano's early beach paintings: Green and Blue, Pink and Saffron, and Trailing Toes, all painted in 1993.

Each shows women by the sea in summer dresses, walking or resting on the sand, parasols raised against the sun. Together they make one of his most relaxed and joyful groups of work, closer in mood to a sunny afternoon than to the noir tension of his interiors.

They were painted in the early nineteen-nineties, as his reputation was growing fast, and they show his early fascination with colour, with costume, and with the shoreline as a stage. The dresses give the series its palette, and the titles take their names straight from those colours. Seen side by side, the three images read like scenes from the same long summer's day.

The triptych has been released as a Special Open Edition, three high-quality giclées on museum etching paper. Framed identically and hung in a row, as a triptych is meant to be seen, they make a wonderful first Vettriano, a lovely gift, or the heart of a summer room.
