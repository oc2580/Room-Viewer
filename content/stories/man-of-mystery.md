Man of Mystery. A woman in a white robe takes a drink from a man's hand.

We see the gesture, but not the story behind it. And that is exactly the point. The man remains a mystery, to us, and perhaps to her as well. Is this a moment of trust, or of temptation? Vettriano will not tell us.

He was a master of the half-told story, and this painting rests entirely on a single, charged moment. Everything is in the hands: the glass offered, the glass taken.

The white of her robe against the darker surroundings gives the picture a strong, clear focus. Your eye goes straight to her, and then to the hand reaching into the frame.

This is a limited edition of two hundred and fifty giclée prints, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, presented in a generous mount that gives it real presence on the wall. Like all his signed editions, its supply is now fixed. It is a favourite with collectors who love the more intimate, narrative side of his work.
