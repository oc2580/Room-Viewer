The Letter. A woman lies on a chaise longue in a quiet room. Beside her, on the floor, a letter has fallen.

What did it say? Was it the letter she had been waiting for, or the one she had dreaded? Has she read it and let it fall, or is she not yet ready to pick it up? Vettriano gives us every clue except the one that matters, and that is exactly why the picture stays with you.

It is a scene of complete stillness, and yet something has clearly just happened. That was his particular gift: the quiet room in the moments after the drama.

The Letter was chosen for a premium signed edition celebrating Jack Vettriano's greatest works, released on the thirteenth of September 2019. It is reproduced at the full size of the original painting, thirty by twenty-four inches, board mounted and finished by hand with three coats of lacquer for a satin surface. It arrives in the same style of frame used for his original work, with no border and no glass.

The edition is tiny: just fifty, plus five Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Vettriano in silver pen.
