Her Secret Life. A woman in a black dress makes a call from a seaside phone box, bunting fluttering in the breeze outside.

Who is on the other end of the line? That is the question the title asks, and it is the question Vettriano wants us to carry away with us. The bunting suggests a holiday, a seaside town in its best clothes. And she has slipped away from it all, to make one private call.

It is a perfect example of how he worked. A single, ordinary moment, a phone box on a promenade, becomes a whole hidden story, simply by giving it the right title and the right light. We are not told what her secret is. We are only told that she has one.

The painting was shown in Love, Devotion and Surrender, Vettriano's exhibition of June 2006.

This framed edition was published in 2006, limited to two hundred and ninety-five giclée prints, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano.
