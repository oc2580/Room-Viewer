The Blue Gown is one of Vettriano's most intimate compositions.

A woman stands before a mirror, holding a blue gown. She is caught in a private moment of choosing, or perhaps of deciding whether to go out at all. The rich blue of the dress against the darker tones of the room gives the picture its focus, and the mirror adds the layer of looking, and of being looked at, that runs through so much of his work.

Vettriano returned again and again to the dressing room and the boudoir, where his figures prepare for an evening whose outcome we never see. These pictures are less about glamour than about anticipation. The Blue Gown is one of the finest examples, and it has become one of the most popular images in our collection.

The edition is one hundred and twenty-five prints and fifteen Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano. It is a generous size for a living room or bedroom wall, without overpowering the space, and it can be supplied framed by the publisher. As a first Vettriano, or as an addition to an established collection, it is a beautiful choice.
