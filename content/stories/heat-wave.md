Heat Wave. The kind of sultry, airless day that slows everything down.

A woman holds a cold bottle to her forehead against the heat. It is the simplest of gestures, and Vettriano turns it into a whole study of mood and atmosphere. His summer paintings are rarely just about the weather. The heat becomes a backdrop for restlessness, for boredom, or for temptation, and the stillness of the scene leaves plenty of room for the imagination.

The palette is strong and warm, and there is a relaxed sensuality to it. It suits a living room or a bedroom, and it pairs beautifully with his beach and summer pictures.

The edition is one hundred and seventy-five prints and twenty Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, and an Artist's Proof is available. It is printed on museum etching paper and set in a generous mount, which gives the image space to breathe and real presence on the wall.
