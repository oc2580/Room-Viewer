Master of Ceremonies. The figure who runs the evening.

The compère. The host. The man who decides when the music starts, and who is introduced to whom. Vettriano was fascinated by social ritual, at the dance hall, the bar, the formal dinner, and the people who orchestrate these occasions have a natural place in his world.

The title carries his familiar note of theatre, as though this scene were one act in a much longer performance, and we have walked in partway through.

The painting belongs to the strand of his work that celebrates evening wear, ceremony, and a wonderfully old-fashioned sense of occasion. It is a natural choice for a dining room, or anywhere you entertain, and it sits well with his other dance-hall and nightlife images.

The edition is just one hundred prints and ten Artist's Proofs, one of the smaller runs among his signed releases, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano. It is also one of the most accessible signed Vettriano editions we offer.
