The Red Room Collection is a folio of five of Jack Vettriano's most daring images, published together for the first time as signed limited editions in 2014.

The five are Devotion; The Great Poet; Heaven or Hell: the Sweetest Choice; 'Round Midnight; and Scarlet Ribbons. Together they represent the most private side of his art, built around desire, around power, and around the theatre of seduction.

Vettriano never shied away from sensuality, and these are the paintings where he went furthest. They are not for every wall. But for the collector who appreciates this side of his work, they are among the most compelling pictures he ever made.

What makes this a significant piece is that it is the complete folio. Owning all five together gives you the series exactly as Vettriano intended it to be seen, and complete sets are considerably scarcer than single prints from the series.

The edition is limited to one hundred and fifty sets, plus thirty Artist's Proofs, each print signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, on Hahnemühle museum etching paper. Framed identically and hung as a group, they transform a private room.
