Evening of Ritual returns to one of Vettriano's favourite themes: the formalities, and the private ceremonies, of the night.

In his world the evening has its own codes. Codes of dress, of behaviour, and of what is left unsaid. His figures seem to follow them with great care. The title suggests a scene that has been played many times before, where everyone knows their part. And yet there is always the possibility that tonight, this evening, will turn out differently.

It is one of the most atmospheric images in our collection, and it rewards a place where it can be looked at often: a living room, a study, a bedroom. It is the kind of picture that seems to change with the light, and with your mood.

The edition is one of the smallest among his signed releases, just one hundred prints and ten Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano. Small editions like this are always the first to become hard to find, all the more so now that no new signed prints can ever be made.
