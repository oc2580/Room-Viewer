Yesterday's Dreams. It is a reflective, bittersweet title, and it sums up so much of Vettriano's appeal.

His paintings are steeped in memory and nostalgia: for a style of dressing, of dancing and of romance that belongs to another era, and for the hopes people once had for themselves. This title suggests someone looking back on a life, or a love, and wondering quietly what became of it.

It is a contemplative picture rather than a dramatic one, and it invites a second look, and then a third.

This is a Premium Limited Edition, reproduced at the full size of the original painting, twenty-four by twenty inches. It is board mounted and finished by hand with three coats of lacquer, giving a satin surface much closer to canvas than paper, and it is presented in the same style of frame used for his original work, with no glass. The edition is tiny: just seventy-five prints and seven Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Vettriano in silver pen. At this size, it holds a living room or dining room with ease.
