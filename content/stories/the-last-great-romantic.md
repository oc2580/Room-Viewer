The Last Great Romantic. A man in a fedora kisses a woman in a yellow dress, in a doorway by the sea.

If you had to choose a single picture to sum up the mood of Vettriano's work, you could do far worse than this. The style: the hat, the dress, the sea beyond. The longing. And a kiss that feels like the final scene of a film, the moment before the credits roll.

The title could describe the man in the painting. Some would say it describes the painter, too. Vettriano spent a career painting romance, never quite innocent and never quite simple, and always with a sense that it might be the last of its kind.

The yellow dress is a beautiful touch, a burst of warmth against the doorway and the sea, drawing the eye straight to the embrace.

The edition is small, one hundred and fifteen prints and fifteen Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, on museum etching paper. It is a lovely, intimate size, and it makes a perfect gift for the romantic in your life.
