In Thoughts of You. A woman in black sits on a white-draped chair, before a tall window, lost in thought.

It is one of Vettriano's stillest images. Nothing is happening, and that is precisely the point. The black of her dress against the white of the chair, the height of the window, the quiet of the room: everything draws us towards her, and towards whoever it is she is thinking of. The title addresses that person directly. In thoughts of you.

Vettriano painted many women alone like this, waiting, remembering, daydreaming, and these quiet portraits are among the most loved of all his work. They are pictures people choose to live with every day.

This image has been released in more than one limited edition over the years. This one is printed on Hahnemühle mould-made paper, whose soft surface suits the gentle tones of the scene, in an edition of three hundred and fifty, plus forty Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano. It is an intimate size, perfect for a bedroom or a reading corner.
