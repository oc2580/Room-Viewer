Afternoon Reverie has a gentle, unhurried title: an afternoon spent drifting into a daydream.

Reverie is a recurring mood in Vettriano's work. He often caught people in the pause between events, lost in thought, or lost in each other. These quieter paintings show a different side of an artist best known for drama and seduction, and many collectors will tell you they are the ones most rewarding to live with.

This is a calm, restful image. It belongs in a bedroom, a sitting room or a study, and it sits beautifully alongside his other contemplative pictures.

The edition is limited to two hundred and fifty prints and twenty-five Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano. It is printed on a heavier museum etching paper than many of his editions, which gives the image real depth. For anyone beginning a collection, it is one of the most approachable ways to own a signed Vettriano.
