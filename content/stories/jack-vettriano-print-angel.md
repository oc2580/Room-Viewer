Angel. A single word, and the kind of title Vettriano chose when he wanted you to bring your own meaning to the picture.

Angels, fallen and otherwise, appear more than once in his work. His collection Fallen Angels gave its name to one of his best-known books. In his hands, the word is rarely innocent. It carries affection, and a hint of irony too, and the figures he painted under such titles tend to be rather more complicated than their names suggest.

Angel works beautifully on its own, or as part of a group of his figure studies. It is a moderate size, which makes it adaptable: a bedroom, a study, or a quieter corner of a living room where it can be looked at closely.

The edition is just one hundred prints and ten Artist's Proofs, one of the smaller runs among his signed releases, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano. As one of our more accessible signed editions, it is a lovely way into his work.
