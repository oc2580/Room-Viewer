Night Calls belongs to the after-dark side of Vettriano's work.

The telephone. The late hour. The closed door. Together, they set the scene for something private. The title alone suggests a call that should not be made, or an arrangement made under cover of night.

These interiors are where his love of film noir shows most clearly: the low light, the strong sense of mood, and a story told through glances and gestures rather than spelled out. He gives us the atmosphere, and lets us write the plot.

It is a picture for the collector who prefers the darker, more cinematic strand of his work to the sunlit beaches, and it brings real atmosphere to a wall.

The edition is just one hundred prints and ten Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, on museum etching paper. An edition of one hundred is small by the standards of his published prints, and now that no new signed editions can be made, the supply is fixed. If you are looking for something rarer still, we also hold an Artist's Proof of this same image.
