Night Calls belongs to the after-dark side of Vettriano's work: the telephone, the late hour, the closed door, and something private about to happen.

The title suggests a call that should not be made, or an arrangement made under cover of night. This is Vettriano at his most noir. Low light, a heavy sense of mood, and a story told through glances and gestures rather than spelled out.

But the print in front of you is something rarer than the image alone. It is not from the main edition. It is an Artist's Proof, and it is numbered one of ten.

Artist's Proofs are the small group of prints traditionally set aside from an edition for the artist himself. They are among the most sought-after prints a collector can own. The main edition of Night Calls was limited to one hundred prints, which means only ten proofs exist in the world, and this is the first of them.

It is signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, on museum etching paper, and comes with its certificate of authenticity. For a serious collector of his work, a proof numbered one is a very special thing.
