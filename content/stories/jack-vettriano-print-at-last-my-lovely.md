At Last, My Lovely. It has the warmth of a song lyric, and the relief of a long wait finally over.

This is a title about reunion: the person you have been waiting for, at last walking through the door. Vettriano's paintings so often hover between romance and melancholy. This one leans, quite openly, towards romance.

The print you are looking at is something special. It is an Artist's Proof. The edition comprised one hundred and twenty-five prints, plus just fifteen proofs, traditionally set aside for the artist himself. That scarcity is why collectors prize them, and this one is signed by Jack Vettriano and comes with its certificate of authenticity.

It is a compact image, printed on heavy museum etching paper, which makes it a lovely choice for a bedroom, or as a gift for someone who has been worth the wait. And it sits very comfortably within a group of his smaller prints.
