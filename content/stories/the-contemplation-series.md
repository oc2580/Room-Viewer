The Contemplation Series is not one print, but five, and together they tell you a great deal about the later Vettriano.

The set brings together Baby, Bye Bye; Black on Blonde; Edinburgh Afternoon; Model in Westwood; and The Very Thought of You. All five were published for the first time as signed limited editions in 2015, and as the name suggests, they share a mood: atmospheric, reflective, and quietly thought-provoking.

These are not his dramatic noir scenes. They are moments of pause. A woman at a window after a farewell. A study in black against fair hair. An afternoon in Edinburgh. A model, posed and aware of being looked at. And a title borrowed from a love song. Hung together, they read like five chapters from the same novel.

What makes this set special for a collector is that the five prints come with matching edition numbers. Each is signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, from an edition of two hundred and fifty, on Hahnemühle museum etching paper. A matched set like this is far harder to assemble than five individual prints, and it makes a remarkable wall.
