Dancer for Money. The title is blunt, and deliberately so.

Vettriano was drawn throughout his career to the world of performers, dancers and the people who watch them: the glamour on the surface, and the transaction underneath. The title tells us exactly which side of that world we are looking at, and leaves us to feel what we will about it.

It was chosen as one of the paintings for a premium signed edition celebrating Jack Vettriano's greatest works, and that tells you how it is regarded.

This premium edition is presented very differently from a paper print. It is reproduced at the full size of the original painting, twenty-four by twenty inches, as a high-quality giclée, board mounted and finished by hand with three coats of lacquer for a satin surface. It arrives in the same style of frame used for his original work, with no border and no glass, ready to hang.

The edition is tiny: just fifty, plus five proofs, each signed and numbered by Vettriano in silver pen in the corner of the image.
