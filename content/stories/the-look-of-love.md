The Look of Love takes its title from the Burt Bacharach song, made famous by Dusty Springfield, and it shares that song's mood exactly: longing, and romance, and a little ache.

Vettriano often found his titles in the music of the nineteen-fifties and sixties. They give his paintings a nostalgic, cinematic quality, as though each one had its own soundtrack. Here, the title asks us to read every glance and every gesture as a sign of love: love given, or love withheld.

Play the song in your head as you look, and the picture comes alive in a different way.

It is one of the larger signed prints on paper in our collection, with real presence, and it belongs in a living room or a bedroom, where it can be enjoyed every day.

The Look of Love was published in 2010 as a limited edition giclée of two hundred and fifty prints, plus twenty-five Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, on heavyweight museum etching paper.
