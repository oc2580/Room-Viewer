Exit Eden is one of Vettriano's most literary titles. It draws on the oldest story of temptation and loss there is: the expulsion from the Garden of Eden.

In his hands that ancient idea becomes modern and personal. It is about the moment innocence ends, or the moment a relationship crosses a line that cannot be uncrossed. So many of his paintings deal with desire and its consequences. This is the one whose title says so outright.

It is also one of the larger images among his signed editions on paper, more than fifty centimetres across, and it has real presence on a wall. Above a fireplace, or as the focal point of a living room, it holds the eye.

The edition is one hundred and seventy-five prints and twenty Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, on museum etching paper, and an Artist's Proof is available. For a collector who wants a statement piece on paper, without moving up to the premium board-mounted editions, it is a compelling choice.
