The Tuiga Collection takes us to Monaco, and to the sea.

In 2009, Vettriano held an exhibition at the Monaco Yacht Club called Homage à Tuiga. It was inspired by the centenary of the club's world-famous yacht, Tuiga. This set of four prints brings together images from that exhibition: Below Deck; Masthead; Ship of Dreams; and Sunshine and Champagne.

It is a side of Vettriano many people do not know. Instead of Scottish beaches and smoky bars, here is the glamour of the Riviera, classic sailing, and long, sunlit days on the water. And yet the titles are pure Vettriano: Ship of Dreams, Sunshine and Champagne. There is romance on board, as there always is in his world.

For a collector, the set has real rarity. It is limited to just seventy-five, plus seven Artist's Proofs, each print signed by Jack Vettriano, and published in 2009.

Framed together, the four make a beautiful group, perfect for a study, a coastal home, or for anyone who loves the sea.
