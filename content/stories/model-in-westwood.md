Model in Westwood brings fashion to the forefront.

Clothes were always central to Vettriano's art. The cut of a suit, the line of a dress, the way a stocking or a glove catches the light: in his paintings, what people wear tells you who they are, or who they want to be. Here, the clothes are the subject, and the title names them.

This painting belongs to his series of studio and model pictures, which explore the relationship between the painter, his sitter, and the costume. The model becomes a figure of style and attitude, posed and aware of being looked at.

It was published in 2015, one of his later editions, in an edition of two hundred and fifty prints and twenty-five Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, on museum etching paper.

It is one of the most accessible signed editions we offer, and with its stylish subject, it is a wonderful choice for a bedroom, a dressing room, or a study.
