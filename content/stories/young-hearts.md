Young Hearts takes Vettriano back to the setting he made his own: the beach.

He grew up on the Fife coast, and the shoreline returns again and again in his paintings, as a stage where people dress up, dance, or simply wait beneath skies that seem to be holding their breath. Here a couple stand together by the sea, and the mood is not the dark, noir tension of his interiors but something gentler: romance, and a touch of nostalgia.

The title points to first love, or at least the memory of it. This is the kind of picture people buy because it reminds them of someone, which is perhaps why his beach paintings have always been among his most loved.

Young Hearts is an official posthumous edition, released after the artist's death in 2025, limited to one hundred prints and ten Studio Proofs. In place of a signature, each one is numbered and embossed with the stamp of the Estate of Jack Vettriano, and it comes with a certificate of authenticity.
