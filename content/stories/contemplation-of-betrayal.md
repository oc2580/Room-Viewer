Contemplation of Betrayal is the quietest painting in Vettriano's betrayal sequence, and perhaps the most telling.

It is dusk, by a window. A man in a dark suit leans in to kiss a woman's cheek. She has a glass in her hand, and she is looking away.

Alongside Betrayal – First Kiss and Betrayal – No Turning Back, this is the third chapter of the story, and its mood is entirely different. There is no embrace here, no line yet crossed. This is the moment of hesitation, before the decision. Everything depends on which way she turns.

That was Vettriano's great skill: finding the instant when a story could still go either way, and holding it there for us. Her averted gaze tells us more than any embrace could.

The edition is small, one hundred prints and ten Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano. For a collector drawn to the betrayal paintings, it completes the trilogy, and for many it is the most compelling of the three.
