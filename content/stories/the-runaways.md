The Runaways. A title full of escape.

Vettriano's paintings are crowded with people caught between one life and another. Arriving at a hotel. Leaving a party. Slipping away together from somewhere they really ought to be. This title gives the image that same feeling, of a story already well under way, and of two people who have chosen each other over everything else.

Where are they running from? And will anyone come after them? As ever, he leaves the ending to us.

It is a compact image, which makes it one of the easiest Vettriano prints to place: on a narrow wall, a landing, a hallway, or as part of a group with his other small-format editions.

The edition is one hundred and seventy-five prints and twenty Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, on museum etching paper, and an Artist's Proof is available. Smaller signed editions like this are often where collectors begin, and they remain a lovely way to add another Vettriano story to a growing collection.
