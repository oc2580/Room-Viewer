Beautiful Dreamer. A woman in a black dress stands beside a window, lost in her own thoughts.

The title comes from Stephen Foster's famous nineteenth-century song, and Vettriano uses it with real tenderness. Many of his finest paintings are quiet portraits of women alone: waiting, remembering, daydreaming. This is one of the most admired of them all. The light from the window and the stillness of the pose give it a calm, reflective mood that stays with you.

It is a large print, and that matters. At this scale its atmosphere can fill a wall, in a bedroom, a living room, or a quiet reading space.

The edition is printed on Hahnemühle mould-made paper. Hahnemühle has been making paper in Germany since the sixteenth century, and its soft surface gives Vettriano's colours an almost painterly finish. Each print is signed and numbered by the artist, from an edition of four hundred and ninety-five, and for its scale and quality it is one of the most impressive works on paper in our collection.
