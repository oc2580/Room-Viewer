Birdy. An affectionate, slightly old-fashioned term of endearment, the kind of word that belongs to the dance halls and pubs of the post-war years Vettriano so loved to evoke.

His titles often borrow from everyday speech, from songs and films, and they give his paintings a conversational, personal quality. It is as if we are being let in on a private nickname, something said quietly between two people.

Birdy is a small, intimate image, and it is a natural fit for the kind of wall many collectors build over time: a gathering of his smaller prints, mixing portraits and narrative scenes. It holds its own alone, and it sits beautifully in a group.

The edition is a modest one, one hundred and twenty-five prints and fifteen Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano and printed on museum etching paper. An Artist's Proof is also available. As a first signed Vettriano, or a thoughtful gift for a collector who already loves his work, it is a lovely choice.
