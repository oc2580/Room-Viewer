Arrangement in Red, White and Blue borrows its title from the language of fine art.

Painters such as Whistler named their pictures in just this way, Arrangement in Grey and Black, treating them as harmonies of colour as much as portraits of people. Vettriano's title nods to that tradition with a knowing smile. The red, the white and the blue are the key to the whole composition, and the title invites you to look at the picture as a study in colour as well as a scene.

That palette gives the image a crisp, graphic quality. It is confident and contemporary, and it holds its own in a modern interior just as well as a traditional one.

This is a Premium Limited Edition, and it is presented very differently from a paper print. It is reproduced at the same size as the original painting, board mounted, then finished by hand with three coats of lacquer for a satin surface, closer to canvas than paper. It arrives in the same style of frame used for his original work, with no border and no glass. The edition is one hundred and twenty-five, plus fifteen Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Vettriano in silver pen.
