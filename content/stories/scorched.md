Scorched. A woman in a black swimsuit lies stretched out on the sand, under a hot sky.

It is one of Vettriano's simplest beach images, and one of his most striking. There is no second figure, no story to untangle, no umbrella or dance partner. Just a single figure, the sand, and the heat. And the title, one word, tells you exactly how that sun feels.

He grew up on the Fife coast, and the beach was his great stage. Here, he strips that stage right back to its essentials, and the result is bold, graphic, and utterly confident.

The original oil was painted in 2012, and it was chosen for Jack Vettriano: A Retrospective, at Kelvingrove Art Gallery and Museum in Glasgow, in 2013 and 2014. The original has since sold.

This print is an Artist's Proof, from an edition of two hundred and fifty prints, published in 2013, with just twenty-five proofs. It is signed by Jack Vettriano, and printed on heavyweight museum etching paper.
