Heaven or Hell comes from the most sensual chapter of Vettriano's work.

Its full title is Heaven or Hell: the Sweetest Choice, and it belongs to the imagery of The Red Room Collection, a folio of five of his most daring paintings, published as signed limited editions in 2014. The title captures the dilemma at the heart of all of them: desire that promises bliss, and risks the consequences, in the very same moment.

Vettriano never shied away from sensuality, and the Red Room images are among his boldest. This is a picture for a private space, a bedroom or a dressing room, and for the collector who appreciates the more daring side of his art.

This is a rare signed limited edition, offered on its own, separately from the five-print folio, which is also in our collection. It is signed by Jack Vettriano and comes with a certificate of authenticity. And since his death in 2025, no new signed prints can be made. The supply of this edition is fixed, and it will not grow.
