The Very Thought of You takes its title from the 1934 Ray Noble song, later recorded by Nat King Cole and so many others.

It is a song about being utterly consumed by thoughts of someone, and Vettriano's painting carries that same dreamy, romantic mood. His love of the great popular standards runs right through his titles. It is as if each painting comes with its own soundtrack, and here you can almost hear it playing.

Published in 2015, this is one of his later editions, from a period when his work often became more intimate and gentle. It is a natural fit for a bedroom or a sitting room, and it makes a beautiful gift for someone you think of in just that way.

The edition is two hundred and fifty prints and twenty-five Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, printed on heavy museum etching paper. It is one of the most accessible signed editions in our collection.
