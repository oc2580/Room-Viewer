Baby, Bye Bye. A woman in a robe stands at a window, a mug in her hand, looking out.

It is the morning after, or perhaps the morning of a departure. The title, with its echo of a pop-song farewell, tells us that someone has gone, or is about to. There is no drama here, no evening dress, no dance floor. Just a quiet, domestic moment that anyone who has ever said goodbye will recognise.

That is what makes it one of Vettriano's most relatable images. Painted in his later period, when his work became more intimate and understated, it shows how much he could say with very little: a pose, a window, the light of the morning.

It was published in 2015, a limited edition of two hundred and fifty prints and twenty-five Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano. As one of his later signed releases, it belongs to a body of work that is now complete. This example comes framed, ready for a bedroom, a kitchen or a sitting room.
