Love Italia is Vettriano on holiday.

A couple ride a white scooter. She wears a white dress with a red sash, he wears a blazer, and behind them stands a large red number seven. It is one of his brightest and most carefree images, full of sunshine and holiday romance.

So much of Vettriano's world happens at night, in smoky bars and dimly lit rooms, under heavy skies. Love Italia is the other side of him: playful, colourful, and unashamedly joyful. The white scooter, the flash of red, the sense of movement: you can almost feel the warm air.

And notice the seven. It appears elsewhere in his work too, the lucky number, and here it seems to promise that luck is on this couple's side.

This is an Artist's Proof, from an edition of one hundred and seventy-five prints with just twenty proofs set aside, signed by Jack Vettriano. Artist's Proofs are prized for their scarcity. It is a compact image, and it brings a burst of Mediterranean light to any room.
