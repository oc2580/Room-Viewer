The Devoted Dressmaker turns to a figure who stands behind the scenes of so much of Vettriano's work.

His paintings are full of beautiful clothes, from evening gowns to tailored suits. And the dressmaker is the person who makes all of that glamour possible. The title is affectionate, and a little mysterious. Devoted to her craft? Or devoted to the person she is dressing?

Clothing was never just decoration for Vettriano. The cut of a dress, a stocking seam, a pair of gloves: these tell us who his characters are, and what they want. This painting puts that lifelong fascination with dress right at its centre.

It was released on the twenty-third of November 2018, one of the later editions published in his lifetime, as a signed limited edition of one hundred and twenty-five prints and fifteen Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano.

It is a small, intimate image, on museum etching paper, and this example comes framed. It belongs in a bedroom, a dressing room, or a study, and it brings a more recent image to any collection.
