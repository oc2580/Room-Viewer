Black on Blonde. It reads almost like a colour note, or a cocktail order, and that blend of style and suggestion is pure Vettriano.

Black clothing against fair hair is a contrast he returned to again and again, especially in his portraits of women. Here he puts that idea front and centre, and the title is the clue. This is a study in elegance, in contrast, and in attitude.

It was published in 2015, alongside several other editions in the same format, and it belongs to his later work: assured, pared back, and very sure of its effect.

The edition is two hundred and fifty prints and twenty-five Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, on museum etching paper. Its moderate size makes it versatile, in a bedroom, a hallway or a study. And as one of the most accessible signed editions in our collection, it is a natural place for a new collector to begin.
