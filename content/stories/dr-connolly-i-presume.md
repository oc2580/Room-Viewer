Dr Connolly, I Presume is a portrait of a Scottish legend by a Scottish legend.

Jack Vettriano was one of three artists, alongside John Byrne and Rachel Maclean, commissioned to portray the comedian Billy Connolly. Their portraits were reproduced as enormous murals in Glasgow, celebrating one of the city's most beloved sons.

Vettriano's answer was characteristically his own. Connolly stands in a long coat on a beach, the setting Vettriano returned to throughout his career, and the title borrows the most famous line of Victorian exploration with a wink. It is a portrait with all the style and theatre of his figure paintings, and a real sense of affection for its subject.

This edition was published in 2017, limited to one hundred giclée prints, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano. It is a small, jewel-like image, and as a meeting of two great Scottish personalities it holds a particular place in any collection of his work.
