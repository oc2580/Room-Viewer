Queen of the Waltzers. It is one of Vettriano's most evocative titles.

The Waltzers is the classic fairground ride: spinning cars, loud music, coloured lights. And the title conjures the young woman who rules it, the one everyone notices as the cars whirl past.

It is a title rooted in the working-class Scotland Vettriano came from, where the dance hall and the fair were where people went to see, and to be seen. He often said that his paintings drew on memory, and on the social world of his youth in Fife, and titles like this carry that sense of time and place.

There is warmth in this picture, and more than a hint of nostalgia. For anyone who remembers the fair coming to town, it will strike a chord.

The edition is one hundred and twenty-five prints and fifteen Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, on museum etching paper. It is a compact image, easy to place in almost any room, and one of the most approachable signed editions in our collection.
