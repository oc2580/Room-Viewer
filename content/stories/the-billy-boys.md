The Billy Boys. A short, punchy title, the kind Vettriano favoured when he wanted us to bring our own story to the picture.

His work is full of figures who carry themselves with style and swagger. Sharp suits, a certain set of the shoulders, the confidence of men who know they are being watched. Titles like this one add a harder, more down-to-earth edge to the elegance he is famous for. These are not drawing-room gentlemen. They are the lads from the dance hall, dressed up for the night.

It is one of the larger signed prints on paper in our collection, with real presence on a wall. It works as a single statement piece in a living room, a study or a hallway, or alongside his other larger signed editions.

The edition is two hundred and seventy-five prints and thirty Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, on heavyweight museum etching paper, and an Artist's Proof is available for the collector who wants the rarer example.
