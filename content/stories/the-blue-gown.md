The Blue Gown. A woman in a blue ball gown before a mirror, studying herself, before the evening ahead.

It is a private, expectant moment, and Vettriano paints it in his richest, most cinematic light. The deep blue of the gown glows against the darker room, and the mirror adds the layer of looking, and of being looked at, that runs through so much of his work.

He returned often to the dressing room, where his figures prepare for an evening whose outcome we never see. These pictures are less about glamour than about anticipation: the breath held before the door opens. The Blue Gown is one of the finest of them, and one of the most popular images in our collection.

The edition is one hundred and twenty-five prints and fifteen Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, on museum etching paper. This example comes framed, ready for a bedroom or a dressing-room wall.
