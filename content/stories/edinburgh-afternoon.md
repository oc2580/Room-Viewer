Edinburgh Afternoon brings Vettriano's world home, to the Scottish capital.

Edinburgh is closely tied to his story. It was at the Royal Scottish Academy, in 1988, that a self-taught painter from Fife submitted two pictures to the annual exhibition, and saw both of them sell on the very first day. That was the moment his career began.

Most of his paintings take place in imagined settings: a bar, a ballroom, a beach that could be anywhere. So a title that names a real city gives this image a rare sense of place, and it has become a favourite with collectors who know and love Edinburgh.

And the afternoon of the title sets a slower, more relaxed mood than his night scenes. This is Vettriano in daylight.

The edition is two hundred and fifty prints and twenty-five Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, on museum etching paper. It makes a wonderful gift for anyone with a connection to the city, and it is an approachable first signed Vettriano.
