Fetish is Vettriano up close.

A man in a dark suit draws on a cigarette. Across the foreground stretches a woman's stockinged leg. That is all we are given, and it is more than enough.

This is one of his charged, close-cropped studies of desire, where what is half-seen says as much as anything shown. He was a master of the edge of the frame, of letting the viewer's imagination complete the picture. Here, the cropping is the story. We see the man, his composure, his cigarette. Of her, we see only what he sees.

It is a bold, cinematic image, very much from the noir side of his work, and it belongs in a private space: a study, a dressing room, a bedroom.

The edition was published in 2016, one hundred and seventy-five prints and twenty Artist's Proofs, each signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano, on museum etching paper. This example comes mounted and framed, ready to hang.
