Night in the City. A couple in a room high above the city, the lights of the streets spread out beyond the window.

It is one of Vettriano's most atmospheric urban paintings, and it combines two things he did brilliantly: the intimacy of a private room, and the scale and glamour of the city at night.

The view is part of the story. It reminds us of the world this couple have shut out, or the life they have stepped away from, just for this evening. Down there are the streets, the traffic, everyone else. Up here, there are only the two of them.

The painting has the polished, cinematic quality of his finest night scenes, and its moody palette makes it a striking presence in a living room, a bedroom or a study.

It was published in 2006 as a limited edition giclée, each print signed and numbered by Jack Vettriano. The image is a generous size, which suits the panoramic feel of the composition, and this example is framed. Twenty years on, the edition is well established, and good examples are increasingly sought after.
